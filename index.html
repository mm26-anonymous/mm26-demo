ControlFoley: Unified and Controllable Video-to-Audio Generation
with Cross-Modal Conflict Handling

Left: Overview of the ControlFoley framework with three multimodal conditioning modes for controllable video-synchronized audio generation. Right: Performance radar chart of Video-to-Audio models.

We propose ControlFoley, a unified and controllable multimodal V2A framework that enables precise control across video, text and reference audio.

Overview of the ControlFoley model architecture.

ControlFoley builds upon a MMDiT structure for controllable V2A generation. We introduce several key innovations to improve multimodal alignment and controllability, particularly under challenging scenarios with inconsistent multimodal inputs.
